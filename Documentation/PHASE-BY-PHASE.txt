# But I recommend we start even smaller

Before **Phase 0**, there's actually one thing I'd like to establish:

## Our TINDAHAN product principle

From everything you've given me, I see three competing priorities:

```text
             TINDAHAN
                │
       ┌────────┼────────┐
       ↓        ↓        ↓
    SIMPLE    VISUAL    PRACTICAL
     UX      IDENTITY    OPERATION
```

**Simple UX** — because the admin is using a cellphone and shouldn't struggle with the system.

**Visual identity** — because you've chosen the glassmorphism direction and want the app to feel polished.

**Practical operation** — because this isn't just a portfolio UI. It needs to actually handle orders, addresses, messages, daily sales, and printing.

The danger is allowing the visual design to overpower the other two.

So my rule for us should be:

> **Glassmorphism is the visual language of TINDAHAN, not the purpose of TINDAHAN.**

If a glass effect makes a button harder to see, **we remove the effect**.

If a beautiful animation slows down order entry, **we remove the animation**.

If a fancy interaction makes the app harder for your mom to understand, **we simplify it**.

That gives us a very clear design philosophy.
